About Page

My name is Dr. Geof Kariuki Kayai, and I am a Kenyan-born surgeon currently practicing in the United Kingdom. Raised in Kenya and educated in some of its most rigorous academic institutions, I developed a strong foundation in science, empathy, and service from an early age. These values followed me through medical school and beyond, shaping the doctor—and person—I have become. My transition to the UK was both a professional leap and a personal evolution, allowing me to grow within one of the world’s most advanced medical environments.

The journey to where I am today has been marked by both challenge and purpose. As a medical student in Kenya, I witnessed first-hand the gaps and struggles faced by patients in resource-limited settings. It inspired me to pursue surgical excellence with the dream of one day giving back in a more meaningful, impactful way. After earning my qualifications, I moved to the UK for further specialization. There, I’ve had the opportunity to work in top-tier hospitals, collaborate with experienced professionals, and expand my surgical expertise across a range of complex procedures.

Despite building a career in a foreign country, my identity and mission remain deeply rooted in Kenya. I have never forgotten where I come from, and I am continuously driven by a desire to uplift my people and contribute to the development of healthcare back home. Whether it's through informal advice, mentorship, or sharing medical resources, I believe every step counts. This sense of purpose is what led me to create this website—a formal space to share my journey, values, and vision with others who care about improving healthcare access and quality in Kenya.

One of the core principles guiding my life is the importance of diaspora engagement in nation-building. The skills and knowledge we acquire abroad are not just for personal advancement—they are tools we must reinvest into our communities. As part of the Kenyan medical diaspora, I view myself as both a product of my homeland and a partner in its growth. Through this platform, I hope to encourage more structured and consistent collaborations between healthcare professionals abroad and local teams on the ground.

This website marks the beginning of a broader journey—one of visibility, connection, and contribution. By sharing my background and inviting others to walk this path with me, I hope to inspire dialogue and action among Kenyans at home and abroad. I welcome messages, ideas, and partnerships from individuals and institutions who believe in the power of collective impact. Thank you for taking the time to learn about me. I look forward to working together toward a healthier, stronger future for Kenya.
