Home Page

Welcome to the official website of Dr. Geof Kariuki Kayai, a dedicated Kenyan surgeon based in the United Kingdom. With a profound commitment to the health and wellbeing of people both at home and abroad, this platform serves as an introduction to my journey in medicine and my growing mission to uplift communities in Kenya through knowledge, connection, and service. I believe that medicine is more than a career—it is a calling, and I am passionate about using my professional experience to make a meaningful impact beyond borders.

As a surgeon practicing in an overseas healthcare system, I have gained advanced training, exposure, and access to innovations that continue to shape the future of medical care. These experiences have equipped me with valuable skills that I am eager to share, especially in ways that benefit under-resourced regions such as those in Kenya. Despite being physically distant, my heart and focus remain close to the communities that raised and shaped me. I am constantly looking for ways to bridge my international experience with the needs and aspirations of my fellow Kenyans.

This website is more than just a professional portfolio—it is a doorway to connection. Over the years, many people have reached out to me through Facebook and other informal channels, seeking guidance, mentorship, or even medical advice. While I am grateful for the trust placed in me, I realized the importance of creating a more formal platform where individuals can learn more about my work, values, and vision. Here, you’ll find updates, insights, and opportunities for collaboration as I continue to find ways to give back and stay involved with communities in Kenya.

My ultimate goal is to foster dialogue and practical partnerships between diaspora professionals and local institutions in Kenya. I envision a future where the expertise gained abroad can directly contribute to the strengthening of healthcare systems back home. Whether it’s through mentorship, surgical missions, resource mobilization, or health education, I believe we all have a role to play in shaping a better future for our people. This website will act as a foundation for these efforts, and a first step toward a more structured approach to outreach and impact.

Thank you for taking the time to visit my page. I invite you to explore, connect, and share this platform with others who might benefit from or contribute to this journey. Whether you are a fellow Kenyan, a medical professional, or someone interested in building community across borders, your interest and support are deeply appreciated. Together, let us continue advancing the ideals of care, compassion, and collaboration that lie at the heart of true healthcare.
